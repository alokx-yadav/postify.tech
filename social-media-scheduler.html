What is a social media scheduler?

A social media scheduler is a tool that creates posts in advance and publishes them automatically at the times you choose. Postify is a social media scheduler for nine platforms: plan your content in one calendar, queue it once, and it goes live on schedule, starting at €9.99 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What is a social media scheduler?
A social media scheduler lets you create posts ahead of time and publishes them automatically at the times you choose. Instead of posting manually every day, you plan content in batches, queue it once, and the scheduler takes it live for you, even while you sleep or focus on other work.
Which platforms does Postify support?
Postify schedules and publishes to nine platforms: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Threads, X (Twitter), Pinterest, and Plative. You can post to a single profile or cross-post everywhere at once, adjusting captions, media, and formats per platform before anything goes live.
How much does Postify cost?
Plans run from €9.99 to €199.94 per month. The Hobby plan at €9.99 a month includes scheduling for all nine platforms, analytics, and a link-in-bio page. Yearly billing lowers every price further, and you can switch plans or cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account settings whenever you like and you are not billed again after that. Checkout shows exactly what you pay and when before you confirm, and you can move between plans at any time as your posting needs change.
Can I schedule Instagram Reels and TikTok videos?
Yes. Postify schedules feed posts, Reels, carousels, and Stories on Instagram, and video posts on TikTok. The composer shows which formats each platform accepts as you build the post, and everything publishes automatically at the time you scheduled, without reminders or manual steps.
Is Postify a good Buffer or Hootsuite alternative?
Postify covers nine platforms with scheduling, analytics, an inbox, and approvals from €9.99 a month, a fraction of Hootsuite’s entry price and more platform coverage than Buffer’s starter tiers. Our Buffer alternative and Hootsuite alternative guides compare plans and features side by side.
Can my team or clients approve posts before they publish?
Yes. You can invite teammates, set per-profile permissions, and route drafts through client review so posts are approved before they go live. Agencies manage multiple client brands from one workspace, and the Agency plan adds white-label reports for sharing results.
Can I use AI with Postify?
Postify never posts anything on its own, but it ships an MCP server that lets your own AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, control your Postify account: drafting, scheduling, and managing posts on your instructions. You keep full control of every profile, permission, and scheduled post.
