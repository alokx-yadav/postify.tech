Questions from creators

Which networks are included?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest and Threads, on every plan.

Is Hobby enough for me?

Hobby is one brand with every network, scheduling and analytics. Pick Starter when you need more posts or a second profile.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your billing settings whenever you like, and you are not billed again after that.

Does Postify post for me automatically?

Yes. Schedule once and Postify publishes at the time you picked, including video.
